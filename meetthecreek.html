Meet the Creek

Meet the Creek is an augmented-reality educational experience designed to promote environmental stewardship. The project uses interactive learning to help people engage with creek environments.

Format

An augmented-reality learning experience built around exploration.

Focus

Creek environments and environmental education.

Purpose

Encourage curiosity, awareness, and environmental stewardship.

Project Overview

The experience brings augmented reality into environmental education, using creek environments as a way to explore stewardship. Its central goal is to make learning about the natural world engaging and encourage care for local waterways.
